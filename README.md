## What you can do

**For employees**
- Check in, take a break and check out from your phone
- Your location is saved with every tap
- Request leave and see your leave balance
- Receive payslips by email
- Add it to your home screen and use it like an app

**For managers and companies**
- Live dashboard: who is in, on break or on leave
- Holiday schedule and individual shifts
- Optional office-area check-in limit
- Late and overtime reports for any date range
- Leave types with yearly balances
- Bulk add your whole team
- Notices and alerts to every phone
- Two-step login and a daily summary email

**Payroll with SSF and EPF**
- Set employee and employer percentages once
- Absent days are deducted automatically
- One payroll table for all staff, printable or saved as PDF
- Payslips sent by email when a salary is marked paid

## Who is it for

Offices, shops, schools, restaurants, clinics and any team in Nepal that wants to replace paper registers and spreadsheets.

## How to start

1. Go to https://www.attendance.work/register
2. Register your company and start the 14-day free trial
3. Add your employees from the dashboard
4. Employees log in and start checking in

New to the app? Read the guides: https://www.attendance.work/guide
